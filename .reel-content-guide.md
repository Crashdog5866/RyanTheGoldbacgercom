### REEL 2: "FOH Gain Staging in 30 Seconds" (30s)
**Format**: At console, demonstrating
**Script**:
- 0-5s: "Gain staging isn't sexy. But it's the difference between a mix that breathes and one that clips at the first chorus." (At console, pointing to preamp)
- 5-12s: "Step 1: Unity gain on the preamp. Hit the channel, watch the meter, back off 6dB." (Demonstrate)
- 12-18s: "Step 2: Fader at unity. All your processing — EQ, comp, gate — works *around* this level." (Show fader at 0)
- 18-24s: "Step 3: Check your master bus. If you're hitting -6dB at the loudest moment, you have headroom." (Show master meter)
- 24-30s: "That's it. Gain stage once. Mix all night. Full breakdown on YouTube." (Point to camera)

**Caption**: FOH fundamentals that save your mix every night 🎛️ Full gain staging tutorial on YouTube. #FOHEngineer #LiveSound #GainStaging #AudioEngineering #MixingTips
